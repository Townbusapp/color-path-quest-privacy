Privacy Policy for Color Path Quest

Effective Date: [26/11/2026]
Developer: [11 Apps]

This Privacy Policy explains how Color Path Quest ("the App") handles information when you play the game on Android. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

No account or personal registration is required. We do not directly collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

All gameplay progress—including unlocked levels, completed levels, stars earned, points balance, remaining hints, saved puzzle boards, and audio/vibration preferences—is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App or clearing App storage removes this local data.

2. Advertising & Consent (Google AdMob & UMP)

Color Path Quest uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded advertisements. To serve and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process certain device and usage data, such as:

  • Android Advertising ID and app set ID
  • IP address (used for approximate location and fraud prevention)
  • Device model, OS version, language, and diagnostic/crash data
  • Ad views, clicks, and reward completion events

Where required by law (such as in the EEA, UK, or applicable US states), the App uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your advertising consent at launch. You can review or update your consent preferences at any time inside the App under Settings → Ad Privacy Options (when applicable in your region), or reset/limit your Advertising ID in your Android device settings.

Learn more about how Google handles data: Google Privacy Policy | How Google uses data from apps.

3. Google Play Services (In-App Review & In-App Updates)

The App integrates official Google Play Core libraries for optional in-app ratings/reviews and app update checks. These interactions are handled directly by Google Play under Google's privacy policy.

4. Offline Play & Permissions

Core gameplay works offline without an internet connection. The App requests only minimal standard Android permissions:

  • Internet & Network State: Used solely for AdMob ads, UMP consent, and Google Play update/review checks.
  • Vibration: Used solely for optional haptic feedback (can be disabled in Settings).

5. Children's Privacy

Color Path Quest is a general-audience puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal data through a third-party service, please contact us so we can assist.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not operate backend user accounts or store your personal data on our own servers, you can delete all local game data at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Color Path Quest → Storage → Clear Data or by uninstalling the App.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.
